# Pre-hackathon checklist - OdooXLDCE 2026

Review these items before each event. Event-specific dates, locations, schedules, and requirements are listed in the event notes and source materials.

- [ ] Read the [rules and FAQs](rules-regulations.md).
- [ ] Confirm the event's schedule, venue, and arrival instructions.
- [ ] Check the event page for required documents, devices, and other essentials.
- [ ] Prepare and test your laptop, development environment, and required accounts.
- [ ] Review the [project-building guidelines](rules-regulations.md#project-building-guidelines).
- [ ] Plan transportation and any personal arrangements not provided by the organizers.
