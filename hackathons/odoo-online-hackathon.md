# Odoo Online Hackathon

- Date: 22 Aug 2026
- Duration: 8 hours
- Format: Online event

## Project focus

The project explored was Globetrotter, a concept designed around travel and exploration needs. The short duration made it ideal for defining a clear value proposition and a lightweight product experience.

## Key angle

The challenge was to turn a broad travel idea into a focused, user-friendly digital experience with practical utility and strong narrative value.

## Takeaway

This event reinforced the value of keeping product scope tight while still creating a memorable and useful concept.
