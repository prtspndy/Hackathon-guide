# Odoo Hackathon 2026 - Gandhinagar

- Date: 2 Sep 2026
- Location: Gandhinagar
- Duration: 24 hours

## Challenge context

This Odoo-focused hackathon emphasized business process automation and productivity tooling. The event was designed for fast, end-to-end product thinking with an enterprise software lens.

## Projects explored

### DealFlow 360
A workflow-centric solution aimed at improving deal and sales operations.

### PeoplePay 360
An HR and payroll solution concept focused on workforce operations and compensation management.

### Urban Furniture Accounting System
A domain-specific accounting solution for urban furniture and related operational processes.

## Outcome

The event highlighted how enterprise-friendly problem framing can turn into compelling product ideas when paired with a strong understanding of real business needs.
