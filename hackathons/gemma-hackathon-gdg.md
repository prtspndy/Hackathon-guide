# Gemma 4 Hackathon

- Date: 2 Aug 2026
- Duration: 4 hours
- Event: GDG Gemma Hackathon

## Project ideas explored

This event focused on building useful AI-enabled tools for day-to-day problems. Several concepts were explored in a short time frame, each targeting a real user need.

### 1. VyaparMitra
A business support concept aimed at helping small traders handle operational tasks more effectively.

### 2. Clinic Intake Assistant
A practical assistant for streamlining patient intake and front-desk processes in clinics.

### 3. KrishiMitra
A self-made agricultural solution concept designed to support farmers with actionable guidance and information.

## Takeaway

The hackathon was a strong example of rapid problem-solving where ideas were shaped around practical user impact, not just technical novelty.
